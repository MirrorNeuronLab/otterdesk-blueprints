# Bibblio parent lifecycle playbook

Map feedback to acquisition expectation, signup, first value, repeat routine, trial conversion, retained use, support, or return. Fix product defects and unclear next steps before adding communications.

Use de-identified cohort and aggregate feedback evidence. Never infer prevalence from a small convenience sample. Draft one relevant adult-directed intervention with a frequency cap, holdout or comparison, stop rule, and support escalation path.

Share aggregate retained-segment patterns and objections with GTM. Do not export raw family records, target children, create pressure loops, or imply unsupported progress.
