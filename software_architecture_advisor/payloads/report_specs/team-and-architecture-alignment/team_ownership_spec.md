# Team ownership

**Section:** 15 · Team and architecture alignment  
**Specification ID:** `AR-15-02`  
**Customer question:** Which team is accountable for each capability, service, and operational outcome?

## What to include

- Map teams or roles to business capabilities, services, data domains, and operational responsibilities.
- Describe responsibility for design decisions, implementation, releases, incidents, and long-term maintenance.
- Identify overlapping authority, missing accountability, and distinctions between build and operate ownership.
- Mark proposed ownership changes as decisions requiring stakeholder agreement.

## Why this matters

Architectural advice becomes actionable when it names the accountable role, not just a file. Customers can use this mapping to resolve ambiguity before a migration or incident exposes it.

## Evidence to use

Use supplied team structures, service ownership records, on-call or operational responsibility records, and confirmed decision processes.

## Expected report output

An accountability matrix with capability or component, design owner, delivery owner, operational owner, and unresolved decisions.

## Completion and quality checks

Do not assume an organization chart fully describes operational ownership. Avoid assigning named people or changing responsibilities without explicit evidence and agreement.

Apply the [shared report conventions](../REPORT_CONVENTIONS.md), including
claim-level traceability, confidence, scope, and explicit handling of missing
evidence. This file specifies required content; it does not assert that an
analysis or test has already been performed.
