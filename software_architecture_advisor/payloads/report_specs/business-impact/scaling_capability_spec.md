# Scaling capability

**Section:** 20 · Business impact  
**Specification ID:** `AR-20-06`  
**Customer question:** What business growth can the architecture support, and what investment would unlock more?

## What to include

- Define growth in terms of users, tenants, requests, data, workflow complexity, or geographic reach relevant to the customer.
- Translate the technical ceiling scenarios into affected business capabilities and service expectations.
- Identify incremental versus structural investments and the trigger for each.
- State uncertainty, demand assumptions, operational implications, and verification before investment.

## Why this matters

Customers need to connect capacity work to opportunities or commitments, not merely hypothetical traffic multipliers. The result should support staged decisions that match actual demand.

## Evidence to use

Use scalability analyses, customer-supplied growth scenarios, service requirements, and measured or modeled capacity with explicit assumptions.

## Expected report output

A growth-enablement table with business scenario, architectural constraint, investment option, trigger, confidence, and validation gate.

## Completion and quality checks

Do not claim the architecture guarantees a revenue or customer growth outcome. Keep projected demand separate from measured supported capacity.

Apply the [shared report conventions](../REPORT_CONVENTIONS.md), including
claim-level traceability, confidence, scope, and explicit handling of missing
evidence. This file specifies required content; it does not assert that an
analysis or test has already been performed.
