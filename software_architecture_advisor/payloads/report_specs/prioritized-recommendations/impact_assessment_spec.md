# Impact assessment

**Section:** 18 · Concrete prioritized recommendations  
**Specification ID:** `AR-18-03`  
**Customer question:** Why does this finding matter to the customer?

## What to include

- Describe the technical consequence and its connection to a business or operational outcome.
- Identify affected users, workflows, teams, or data and the scope of exposure.
- Separate measured impact, estimated impact, and plausible risk scenarios.
- State assumptions, relevant time horizon, and the consequence of doing nothing.

## Why this matters

Impact explains why a recommendation deserves capacity compared with competing product work. It also helps the customer avoid treating a large graph metric as a business problem without a demonstrated consequence.

## Evidence to use

Use workflow criticality, change history, incident evidence, delivery records, supplied costs, and customer-stated requirements.

## Expected report output

An impact statement with mechanism, affected outcome, magnitude or qualitative range, evidence basis, assumptions, and no-action consequence.

## Completion and quality checks

Do not invent revenue loss, incident probability, or savings. A qualitative impact can be useful when it is explicit about the evidence and uncertainty.

Apply the [shared report conventions](../REPORT_CONVENTIONS.md), including
claim-level traceability, confidence, scope, and explicit handling of missing
evidence. This file specifies required content; it does not assert that an
analysis or test has already been performed.
