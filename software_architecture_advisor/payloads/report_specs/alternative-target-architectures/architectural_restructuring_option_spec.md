# Architectural restructuring option

**Section:** 17 · Alternative target architectures  
**Specification ID:** `AR-17-03`  
**Customer question:** What broader redesign could address long-term structural requirements?

## What to include

- Define the target capabilities and constraints that smaller interventions cannot adequately meet.
- Describe the proposed components, state ownership, interactions, deployment shape, and operating model.
- Explain migration complexity, coexistence, new infrastructure, and organizational obligations.
- State expected long-term benefit, major uncertainties, and staged investment or stop decisions.

## Why this matters

Customers need a realistic picture of the ambitious option, including what makes it worth considering and what could make it uneconomic. A target diagram alone hides the difficult transition work.

## Evidence to use

Use growth and reliability scenarios, current constraints, business priorities, alternative options, and migration feasibility analysis.

## Expected report output

A target-architecture option with before/after views, requirement coverage, transition stages, effort range, risks, and decision gates.

## Completion and quality checks

Do not imply a rewrite is inevitable or assume projected scale will occur. Explain the requirements that justify the restructuring and preserve a smaller intervention baseline.

Apply the [shared report conventions](../REPORT_CONVENTIONS.md), including
claim-level traceability, confidence, scope, and explicit handling of missing
evidence. This file specifies required content; it does not assert that an
analysis or test has already been performed.
