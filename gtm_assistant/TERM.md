# GTM Assistant Terms

This blueprint produces analysis, drafts, and approval queues. It does not contact customers. Copy, cohorts, frequency caps, and support escalation rules require approval. Its Job response service receives only sanitized Job context and approved Job knowledge; raw customer records, logs, credentials, addresses, message bodies, and unrestricted artifacts are excluded. The unchanged Bibblio demo happens to model a family product.

Bundled data is synthetic. Operators are responsible for using authorized and lawful inputs, minimizing personal data, and obtaining qualified legal, tax, privacy, child-safety, educational, financial, brand, or accessibility review where appropriate.
