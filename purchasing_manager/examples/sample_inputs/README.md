# Purchasing Manager Sample Inputs

Edit `purchase_request.txt` as an internal purchase request: explain the
business need, landed-cost budget, decision horizon, technical and commercial
requirements, numerical planning assumptions, decision owners, and public
research that has already started. The bundled request models an engineering
team sourcing one supportable local-AI desktop for a Boston office under a
$5,000 landed-acquisition ceiling.

The input demonstrates plain-text request intake, requirement extraction,
source provenance, availability and hard-constraint gates, landed acquisition
cost, discounted and risk-adjusted three-year TCO, equivalent annual cost,
productive-hour economics, scenario sensitivity, acquisition-method gaps,
approval planning, local evidence retrieval, and review-only output.

`observed_ai_systems.json` contains time-stamped observations from real public
supplier, manufacturer, government, and component-specification pages. It does
not contain invented quotes. Public listings are volatile and are not offers,
inventory reservations, or price guarantees; open every cited URL again and
obtain a written quote before approval.

The request labels utilization, internal labor, downtime, maintenance reserve,
discount rate, and residual value as company planning assumptions. They are not
supplier claims. Replace them with approved company inputs before relying on the
economic result. Financing and leasing remain `not modeled` until a real lender
or lessor provides complete eligible terms.

Replace or edit `purchase_request.txt`, then add approved local evidence such
as refreshed written quotes, requirements documents, supplier terms, warranty
documents, security review notes, delivery plans, financing/lease term sheets,
or comparison spreadsheets. Use only public research URLs and do not include
secrets or documents that the configured runtime is not authorized to process.
