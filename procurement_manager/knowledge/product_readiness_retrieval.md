# Purchase Research Product Readiness Retrieval

Use this checklist when reviewing the generated purchase packet.

- Is the purchase type, item or trip, budget, location or route, timing, and priority set explicit?
- Are local documents identified by path, hash, extraction method, and source reference?
- Are public queries privacy-safe and free of raw private document text?
- Are observed prices, availability, policies, fees, dates, and source statuses preserved deterministically?
- Are sourced facts, company planning assumptions, and calculated outputs visibly separated?
- Is landed acquisition cost separated from asking price, with mandatory options, tax, fulfillment, and internal implementation itemized?
- For heavy assets, are horizon, discount rate, recurring cost, residual value, financial NPV TCO, risk-adjusted NPV TCO, EAC, productive-unit cost, and low/base/stress sensitivity present or explicitly not modeled?
- Are cash, financing, lease, rental, subscription, and managed-service methods compared only when complete real terms exist, with missing terms explicit otherwise?
- For public listings and supplier quotes, are exact configuration, source URL, observation timestamp, quote date and expiry when applicable, tax, delivery/setup scope, warranty, support, lead time, and commercial exceptions separately recorded?
- Does every preferred option pass the declared technical, budget, delivery, and warranty constraints without treating an unverified field as a pass?
- Are blocked, stale, conflicting, login-required, robots, and CAPTCHA sources recorded as warnings?
- Do RAG citations identify the retrieved guidance or approved local document?
- Does the recommendation use only the five bounded labels and show confidence, risks, gaps, and next steps?
- Does the final report include a procurement decision summary and approval checklist while clearly blocking buying, booking, payment, offers, applications, and seller/provider contact?
