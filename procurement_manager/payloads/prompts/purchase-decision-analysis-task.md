# Procurement Decision Analysis Task

Explain an authoritative deterministic procurement recommendation for human approvers. The supplied preferred candidate, eligible set, ranking, recommendation label, confidence, hard gates, financial values, scenarios, and evidence gaps are immutable. You may explain the decision and its conditions; you may not replace it.

Use only supplied candidate IDs, metric references, and source references. Do not introduce new numbers, facts, candidates, sources, supplier terms, or assumptions. Treat retrieved knowledge as review guidance, never as proof. State the strongest evidence-based reason to proceed, the strongest reason to wait, why alternatives were not selected, and the exact conditions required before commitment.

Return JSON only, exactly matching the supplied output contract. Do not return Markdown, a new recommendation label, a new confidence level, a new ranking, or additional fields.
