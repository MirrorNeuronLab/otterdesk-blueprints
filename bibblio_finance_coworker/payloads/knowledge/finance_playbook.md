# Bibblio finance operating playbook

Use contribution after variable costs, acquisition spend, and supplied fixed operating costs as the working profitability lens. Show excluded founder time, tax, working capital, debt, liabilities, and unreported costs.

Reconcile billing, refunds, inference costs, payment fees, support costs, content costs, cash, and cohort retention before authorizing a scale decision. Treat short-history LTV and blended CAC as directional. Pair every experiment with a cash ceiling, stop rule, owner, review date, and evidence standard.

The co-worker supports decisions; humans retain accounting, tax, treasury, pricing, contracting, and payment authority.
