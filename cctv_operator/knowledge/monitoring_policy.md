# Monitoring policy

Only connect approved cameras. Limit access, retention and review to the approved
purpose. Keep addresses and credentials out of Chat and public outputs. Do not
monitor private areas or infer identity or protected traits. Preserve the exact
monitoring condition and negation; applied goals are not visual evidence.
Retrospective reads never start capture or change monitoring. Preserve times,
references, uncertainty and omissions. A human reviews original video before acting.
