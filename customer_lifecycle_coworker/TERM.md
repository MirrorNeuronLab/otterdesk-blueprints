# Bibblio Parent Lifecycle Co-worker Terms

This blueprint produces analysis, drafts, and approval queues. It does not contact families. Copy, cohorts, frequency caps, and support escalation rules require approval, and raw family records are not shared through MCP.

Bundled data is synthetic. Operators are responsible for using authorized and lawful inputs, minimizing personal data, and obtaining qualified legal, tax, privacy, child-safety, educational, financial, brand, or accessibility review where appropriate.
