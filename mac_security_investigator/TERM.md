# Terms and investigation limits

Review-only security evidence. Findings are not a guarantee of safety, complete
history, malicious intent, continuous protection or forensic admissibility.
Source digests detect later changes to retained copies; they do not attest that
an already compromised source was truthful. You authorize the configured source
scopes. The co-worker never remediates or widens access to overcome a denial.

Keep private evidence on a standalone local runtime. Exports are explicit local
outputs. Enabling cluster replication or remote chat processing is outside the
locality contract. Synthetic examples are fictional and contain no live findings.
