# Bibblio GTM operating playbook

Rank relevance before reach. Treat a seed record as a research lead, not consent. Prefer small founder-reviewed cohorts of educators, family-learning communities, creators, newsletter operators, schools, libraries, and aligned partners. Separate customer discovery from investor learning.

Every outreach candidate needs an adult-professional identity, a plausible Bibblio-relevant reason, a lawful outreach basis, a human-reviewed draft, and an opt-out path. No child-directed marketing, purchased-list automation, unsupported learning claims, bulk sends, or sender-identity ambiguity.

Measure qualified conversations, parent interviews, partner introductions, activation evidence, reply quality, opt-outs, and downstream retained-family value—not raw send volume.
